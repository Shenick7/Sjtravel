Jain food in Bangkok

Say the full rule every time. A sign that looks vegetarian is not a promise that the pot skipped onion, garlic, potato and egg. This page does not list restaurants, because we could not verify a current Jain menu on a restaurant’s own site during this check.

The sentence

English: “Jain food, please. No onion, no garlic, no potato or other root vegetables. No egg. Cook it fresh, do not pick the onion out of a dish that is already made.”

Hindi, if the cook is from India: “Jain khana chahiye — bina pyaaz, bina lehsun, bina kand-mooli, bina anda. Fresh banao.”

Add anything your own family also avoids. Kitchens do not share one definition of Jain.

Jay is not automatically Jain

Some Bangkok restaurants mark a meat-free menu with เจ (jay). Treat that as a reason to ask, not as a finished answer. Ask whether the dish has egg, milk, onion or garlic. Do not assume the cook’s rule matches yours.

A HappyCow listing checked on 17 Nov 2026 says the Govinda’s next to ISKCON at Siyak was reported closed in January 2025. This page will not send you there.

Show the English sentence on your phone if there is any doubt. For a family, call ahead rather than arriving hungry at 14:00.
