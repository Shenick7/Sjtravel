Best Time to Visit Thailand with Family from Chennai

The best time to visit Thailand with family from Chennai is December to February. Those are the coolest, driest months — Bangkok averages just one rain day in December and January, Phuket’s seas are calmest, and the weather suits children and grandparents alike.

When to goBy Shenick Jain16 November 20267 min read

Quick facts

  • Best months: December to February — the cool season, driest.
  • Cheapest months: May to October — the rainy season, when hotels are cheaper.
  • Wettest month: September — Bangkok averages 18 rain days.
  • Flight: about three and a half hours nonstop, Chennai to Bangkok.
  • Entry: 30 days visa-free for Indian passports, plus the free TDAC form.
  • Family budget: about ₹1.1–1.7 lakh land costs for 4, 6 days — flights extra.

Which months are actually the best for a family?

Thailand has three seasons: cool November–February, hot March–May, and rainy from May. For a family with children and parents, the cool season wins clearly — dry days, lower humidity, evenings you can walk in.

Do not expect cold weather. Even in the cool season, Bangkok’s average high is still 31°C to 32°C. What changes is the rain and the night: rain days drop to one or two a month in December and January, nights averaging about 21°C.

December and January are peak season — flights and hotels cost the most. If your dates are flexible, pick February: still dry, Phuket’s fewest rain days of the year, and the Christmas–New Year rush is over.

Ko Samui runs a different calendar: wettest October to December (November averages 489.6 mm), drier February to April. If your trip is only Samui, do not use Phuket’s calendar.

When do Indian school holidays fall — and should I travel then?

For most families the school break decides the trip. Summer break usually falls somewhere between mid-April and end June, depending on your state and board — check your state’s dates.

The catch: April and May are the hottest stretch — April averages 34.9°C in Bangkok. If summer is your only window, pick the beach over the city, do temples early, rest in the afternoon heat. Songkran falls on 13–15 April 2027 — children love it, but book transport and hotels early.

The Christmas–New Year school break lands in the best weather — and the busiest, most expensive week. Book flights six to eight weeks ahead. September is the weakest month for Thailand: it is the wettest month.

What about the islands — is Phuket good all year?

Not all year. The Andaman coast — Phuket, Krabi, Phi Phi — is best November to April. From May to September Phuket averages 19 to 23 rain days a month, the sea is rough, and boat trips can cancel. Keep one day free as backup; never put a non-refundable boat tour on your only full day.

Some islands close on fixed dates: Similan and Surin from 16 May to 14 October, Maya Bay from 1 August to 30 September. Check the closure list before you plan a day around an island.

The Gulf coast is the mirror image: Ko Samui, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao are drier February to September, wettest October to December. If your only leave is July or August, the Gulf side is the drier bet. Pattaya, near Bangkok, is best December to February, no domestic flight needed — good for families with elders.

What will a 6-day family trip cost in rupees?

Planning estimates from our Thailand kit: about ₹1.1–1.7 lakh land costs for a family of 4, 6 days — flights extra. A guide, not a quote — international flights are live fares. Swap Phuket for Pattaya and the same 6 days can cost ₹25,000–40,000 less — no domestic flight, lower hotel rates.

The month and the hotel move the total most. Shoulder months — late October or March — mean lower hotel prices with mostly dry weather.

What about Jain food in Thailand?

Thailand has one hidden problem for Jain families: almost every Thai dish uses fish sauce, oyster sauce or shrimp paste, and Thai vegetarian food is not Jain food. Jay means a strict festive vegetarian style — it helps, but Jay food can still be cooked in shared oil, and you must still say no onion and no garlic.

Eat main meals in Indian pure-veg restaurants — the kit names them by area. Message the restaurant ahead — most Indian kitchens will cook Jain food fresh if they know you are coming.

Say this every time: “Jain food, please. No onion, no garlic, no ginger, no potato, no root vegetables. No egg, no fish sauce, no oyster sauce, no shrimp paste.” In Thai: “Aahaan Jay, kha. Mai sai hom, mai sai kra-tiam, mai sai khing, mai sai man-farang, mai sai khai, mai sai naam-plaa, mai sai naam-man-hoi.” For tours and hotel breakfasts, confirm the Jain meal in writing a day before. On boat days, don’t depend on the tour lunch: eat a full Jain breakfast, carry thepla, khakhra and fruit, and plan a proper Jain dinner back in town.

What should I check before I book?

  • Entry: Indian passports enter visa-free for tourism for up to 30 days, since 15 September 2026. A 6-to-10-day family holiday is well inside the limit. Check the official site before you apply — rules have changed before.
  • TDAC: fill the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card online within 72 hours before landing, for every traveller including children, only at tdac.immigration.go.th. Any site charging for it is fake.
  • Documents: passport valid 6+ months, return ticket, hotel proof, and proof of funds — about THB 20,000 per person or THB 40,000 per family. Our Thailand visa guide has the full checklist.
  • Flights: the nonstop from Chennai to Bangkok takes about three and a half hours. Check live fares on our flight search — compare December against late October before you decide.
  • Weather: check the Thai Meteorological Department forecast the week you fly — averages are a guide, not a forecast for your dates.

What I’d do

If my family could pick any week, I’d take the second week of February — dry, calm seas, past the Christmas rush. If the budget is tight, late October: rain easing, Similan reopens 15 October, hotels cheaper.

Questions, answered

Is December the best month to visit Thailand from Chennai?

Yes, for weather: Bangkok averages one rain day in December and nights near 21°C, and Phuket is drying out. It is peak season, so book flights and hotels six to eight weeks ahead.

Can we visit Thailand in the rainy season with children?

Yes, if you plan around it: showers are usually short and in the afternoon, so keep one spare day and check the forecast daily. Pick the Gulf coast in July and August — it is drier than Phuket then. September is the wettest month, so avoid it if you can choose.

Do Indians need a visa for Thailand?

No, for a holiday of up to 30 days on an ordinary Indian passport, since the rule changed on 15 September 2026. You still need the free TDAC form within 72 hours before landing. Check the official site before you apply.

Is Thailand good for Jain vegetarians?

Yes, if you eat at Indian pure-vegetarian restaurants in Bangkok, Phuket and Pattaya and confirm Jain preparation a day ahead. Thai “Jay” food is vegetarian but not automatically Jain — always say no onion, no garlic and no fish sauce.
