Thailand Honeymoon Itinerary: 6 Days with Budget

This Thailand honeymoon itinerary covers 6 days — three nights in Bangkok and two in Phuket — for two people flying from Chennai, with a rupee budget built from fares and prices seen on 20 November 2026. Nothing here is a fixed price — fares move daily.

ItinerariesBy Shenick Jain20 November 20266 min read

Quick facts

  • The plan: 6 days, 5 nights — Bangkok (3 nights), then Phuket (2 nights), home via Bangkok.
  • Flights: IndiGo nonstop Chennai–Bangkok return from ₹26,379 per person, seen 20 November 2026 (no exact 6-day return was listed that day).
  • Budget: about ₹1,01,000–1,39,000 for two, before food, shopping and insurance.
  • Visa: 30-day visa-free stay for Indians; free TDAC within 72 hours before arrival.
  • Best months: November to February.
  • Exchange rate: 1 THB ≈ ₹2.75 mid-market, seen 20 November 2026.

Day 1 — Arrive in Bangkok

Most Chennai–Bangkok nonstops land at Suvarnabhumi (BKK) in the morning. Into the city: the Airport Rail Link costs ฿45 per person to Phaya Thai and takes 26–30 minutes, or a metered taxi is typically ฿300–550 all-in. Keep day one light — check in, walk the river, eat early and sleep.

Day 2 — The old city: palace and temples

Do the three big temples in one morning, in this order: the Grand Palace (฿500 for foreign visitors, 8:30–16:30, tickets until 15:30), then Wat Pho a short walk south (฿300, the 46-metre reclining Buddha), then a short ferry to Wat Arun (฿200, best late in the day). The three together cost ฿1,000 per person. Cover shoulders and knees — the palace turns people away at the gate. Start at opening, before the coaches arrive.

Day 3 — Bangkok, slowly

Keep day three unplanned. On a weekend, Chatuchak Market is the classic morning out. Otherwise pick one thing — a canal boat ride, a cooking class, or a Thai massage at the Wat Pho school — and leave the rest open.

Day 4 — Fly to Phuket

Fly Bangkok to Phuket in the morning — about 1 hour 20 minutes, and budget carriers typically charge ฿1,200–2,500 one-way per person. Base yourself in Patong if food matters (see below) and spend the afternoon on the beach. The flight, the transfer and the heat are enough for one day.

Day 5 — Phi Phi by speedboat

The one big day out. A Phi Phi speedboat trip typically costs ฿1,845–2,600 per adult, plus a ฿400 national park fee if it is not included — check before you pay. It usually covers Maya Bay, snorkelling stops and lunch, and takes most of the day. Maya Bay landings reopened on 1 October 2026 after the annual closure, but confirm at booking.

Day 6 — Home, via Bangkok

A slow morning in Phuket, then fly Phuket–Bangkok and connect to Chennai. Leave a generous gap between flights — a delayed domestic leg and a tight connection is what ruins a honeymoon. If your Chennai flight leaves Bangkok early, fly back on day 5 evening and sleep near the airport.

Jain food on a honeymoon

Sort food before you fly, not after you land. In Phuket, Patong has pure-vegetarian Indian restaurants that cook Jain meals — the full list is in our Jain food in Phuket article. In Bangkok, say the full Jain requirement every time: a Thai jay (เจ) sign is not automatically Jain food — jay cooking allows potato and carrot and skips milk and paneer, while Jain food is usually the other way round. Our Jain food in Bangkok guide gives the exact sentence to say. Carry dry snacks from home for the gap between meals.

Entry rules: visa-free and the TDAC

Indian passport holders get a 30-day visa-free stay for tourism — the rule in force since 15 September 2026, replacing the earlier 60-day exemption. There is no visa fee. You must still fill in the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC): free, mandatory for all non-Thai nationals, submitted online within 72 hours before arrival, on the official site only. The published scheme also says travellers should carry the equivalent of 20,000 baht per person (40,000 per family).

When to go

November to February is the window. Bangkok is cool and dry, and on the Andaman coast the monsoon is over and the sea is calmest from December to March — which is what makes the day-5 boat trip work. In the rainy months the boat trip can change route or cancel outright.

Questions, answered

How much does a 6-day Thailand honeymoon cost from Chennai?

About ₹1,01,000–1,39,000 for two, before food, shopping and insurance — from fares and prices seen 20 November 2026. International flights are the biggest line (₹52,758 for two).

Do Indians need a visa for a Thailand honeymoon?

No. Indian passport holders get a 30-day visa-free stay for tourism, in force since 15 September 2026. You must still fill in the free TDAC digital arrival card online within 72 hours before arrival.

Is Jain food available on a Thailand honeymoon?

Yes, with planning. Patong has pure-vegetarian Indian restaurants that cook Jain meals. In Bangkok, say the full Jain requirement every time — a jay sign is not automatically Jain food — and call ahead. Carry dry snacks from home.

What is the best month for a Thailand honeymoon?

November to February. Bangkok is cool and dry, and the Andaman sea around Phuket and Phi Phi is calmest from December to March — the best window for the boat trip.
