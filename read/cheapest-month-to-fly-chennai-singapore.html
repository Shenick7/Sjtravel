Cheapest Month to Fly Chennai to Singapore

February is the cheapest month to fly Chennai to Singapore — a return from ₹20,424, and ₹18,956 on Scoot when we opened the results for 1–8 February. December is the dearest month we checked, from ₹31,400. Fares seen on Google Flights on 18 November 2026; they will change.

Flights & moneyBy Shenick Jain18 November 20266 min read

Quick facts

  • Cheapest month: February 2027 — return from ₹20,424; ₹18,956 on Scoot when the results were opened, 1–8 February.
  • Dearest month checked: December 2026 — return from ₹31,400.
  • What February saves: ₹10,976 a person against December — about ₹44,000 for a family of four.
  • Direct flight: about 4 hours 15 minutes. Scoot, IndiGo, Singapore Airlines and Air India Express fly direct.
  • Weather bonus: February is also Singapore’s driest month — the cheap month is a good month to go.

The short answer: February

We checked return fares from Chennai to Singapore for every month from December 2026 to June 2027 on Google Flights, on 18 November 2026. February 2027 came out cheapest, at ₹20,424 for a 7-day return trip departing 1 February. March and January were close behind, at ₹21,226 and ₹21,474. December was far ahead of the rest, at ₹31,400 — about 54% more than February.

The gap is big enough to plan around. A family of four flying in February instead of December saves about ₹44,000 on flights alone, at the fares we saw. That is most of a hotel stay in Singapore.

Why February is cheapest

February sits in the quiet stretch after the year-end rush. Christmas, New Year and the December school break push December fares up, and by February most families are back home and back at work and school. Demand falls, and fares follow. January and March sit close to February for the same reason — the whole January-to-April stretch is the calm, cheaper part of the year on this route.

February also happens to be the best month to actually be in Singapore. It is the city’s driest month, averaging 105.1 mm of rain against 331.9 mm in December, and temperatures sit around 25–32°C all year, so there is no cold season to wait out. The cheapest month and the driest month are the same month — that is rare, and worth using.

Why December and May cost more

December is the most expensive month we checked, and it is not close: ₹31,400 against February’s ₹20,424. Christmas and New Year, the school winter break and Singapore’s festive season all land in the same weeks, and every seat is wanted. Most late-December departures we saw were ₹35,000–₹39,500.

May is the second-dearest, at ₹26,495 — about 30% above February. Summer school holidays start, and families who could not travel in December book May instead. June stays dear for the same reason, which is why its cheapest fare hides at the very end of the month, when the holiday rush starts to thin out.

What the cheapest February trip actually looks like

Opening the results for 1–8 February 2027 showed an even lower fare than the calendar: ₹18,956 return with Scoot on both legs, both nonstop. Outbound leaves Chennai at 11:50 PM on 1 February and lands at 6:35 AM on 2 February — 4 hours 15 minutes. The return leaves Singapore at 9:15 PM on 8 February and lands at 10:50 PM — 4 hours 5 minutes. The calendar price and the opened-result price can differ, so always open the results before deciding.

Four airlines fly the route direct: Scoot, IndiGo, Singapore Airlines and Air India Express. The direct flight takes about 4 hours 15 minutes, and there are departures through the day and late at night. A late-night departure like Scoot’s saves a hotel night on the way out — you sleep on the plane and wake up in Singapore.

How to check the live fare before you book

  • Compare like with like — the same trip length, the same number of travellers, the same bags. A 7-day return and a 3-day return are different prices.
  • Use the calendar view on Google Flights, or the cheapest-dates calendar on our flight search, and move month by month. One month’s cheapest day can be a third cheaper than the month next to it.
  • Open the results. The calendar price is a guide; the fare shown when you open a date can be lower, as February’s ₹18,956 was against the calendar’s ₹20,424. Check the “Cheapest” view, not only “Best”.
  • Re-check on the day you pay. Every fare in this article was seen on 18 November 2026 and will change. Never pay a fare you have not re-checked that day.
  • Count the whole trip. A cheap flight in the wettest month can cost more in spoiled plans than it saves. February wins on fare and on weather.

Don’t forget the visa

Singapore is not visa-free for Indian passport holders, and there is no visa on arrival — the visa must be approved before you fly. Applications go through an authorised visa agent, and the ICA processing fee is SGD 30, about ₹2,000, plus the agent’s own charge. Every traveller must also file the free SG Arrival Card within three days before arrival. The only exception is the 96-hour Visa Free Transit Facility, for travellers passing through Singapore to a third country with a qualifying visa. How long is the flight from Chennai to Singapore?

Do Indians need a visa for Singapore?

Yes — an entry visa approved before you fly, applied for through an authorised visa agent. There is no visa on arrival for Indian passport holders. The ICA fee is SGD 30, about ₹2,000, plus the agent’s charge, and every traveller files the free SG Arrival Card within three days before arrival.
