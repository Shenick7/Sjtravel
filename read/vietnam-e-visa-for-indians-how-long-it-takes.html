Vietnam e-Visa for Indians: How Long It Takes

An Indian passport holder flying to Vietnam needs an e-visa before departure — no visa-free entry and no visa on arrival for a holiday. The official answer is 3 working days, but the Embassy of Vietnam in India says to allow 5 to 30 working days — so apply at least a week, and ideally a month, before you fly.

Visa & entryBy Shenick Jain24 November 20266 min read

Quick facts

  • Do Indians need a visa? Yes — an ordinary Indian passport gets no visa-free entry and no visa on arrival for a holiday.
  • How long it takes: officially 3 working days; the Embassy of Vietnam in India says 5–30 working days.
  • Cost: USD 25 single entry (about ₹2,325) or USD 50 multiple entry (about ₹4,650); non-refundable.
  • Validity: up to 90 days, single or multiple entry — you choose when you apply.
  • Passport: valid at least 6 months after the date you arrive.
  • Apply only at: evisa.gov.vn (also thithucdientu.gov.vn) — every other website is an intermediary.

Do Indians need a visa for Vietnam?

Yes. An ordinary Indian passport holder needs a visa before travelling. The e-visa is the standard route: open to citizens of all countries, applied for entirely online, with no embassy visit and no agent needed.

Do not count on the 45-day visa-free entry. Vietnam gives 45 days without a visa to citizens of 24 countries — the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Russia, Japan and South Korea, plus twelve more European countries for tourism. India is not on either list, so there is no 45-day visa-free option for Indian passport holders. The one exception is Phu Quoc island, covered below. An e-visa is a pre-entry permission, not a promise of entry: officers at the checkpoint decide whether you enter.

How long does the Vietnam e-visa take?

Two official numbers exist, and the gap between them is the point. The Vietnam Immigration Department’s own application page states: “Your application will be processed in 3 working days” after you submit the form and pay the fee. That is the time for a clean, complete application in a normal week.

The Embassy of Vietnam in India is more cautious. Its e-visa page says an e-visa “is processed within 05 – 30 working days” after the Immigration Department receives your completed application and fee, and it advises applying “at least 7 – 40 working days or more prior departure”. Delays happen when information is missing or wrong, so check your registered email regularly after applying.

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding Vietnamese public holidays. There is no official express service, and no agent can make the Immigration Department work faster. Apply as soon as your dates are fixed, and wait until the e-visa is in hand before booking anything non-refundable.

Validity begins from the entry date you asked for, not the day it is approved. You can enter on any date inside that period, but not before it starts or after it ends, and entering later does not extend the exit date.

What documents do you need?

Every traveller needs their own e-visa, including children on their own passport — file the family together so the validity dates line up.

  • Passport: valid at least 6 months after the date you arrive, with a scan of the full data page, including the photo, personal information and the ICAO lines at the bottom.
  • Portrait photo: 4 cm by 6 cm, white background, straight looking, without glasses.
  • Travel details: your intended entry date, the validity period you want (up to 90 days), single or multiple entry, and the checkpoint where you plan to enter and exit.
  • Payment: a card that works for an online payment in US dollars.

Type your name exactly as in the ICAO line at the bottom of your passport data page — a mismatch is one of the commonest reasons a file comes back. If any information is missing, incorrect or unidentified, the Immigration Department states that the application will not be accepted.

How to apply, step by step

You apply yourself, directly to the Immigration Department. No agent is needed, and the embassy warns that all websites other than the two official domains are invalid.

  • 1. Type the address yourself. Go to evisa.gov.vn — also reachable at thithucdientu.gov.vn. Skip sponsored results and look-alike sites: the real portal ends in .gov.vn, under the Ministry of Public Security’s Immigration Department.
  • 2. Fill in the form — passport details, travel dates, single or multiple entry, and your entry and exit checkpoints.
  • 3. Upload the two images — the passport data page and the portrait photo — and check every field before submitting.
  • 4. Pay USD 25 or USD 50 online and save the registration code the system gives you. You will need it to check your result.
  • 5. Check the result yourself. Enter your registration code, email and date of birth on the portal’s search page. No email is sent about the result — and if approved, download and print the e-visa to carry with your passport.

Where can you enter with an e-visa?

An e-visa works at 83 designated international checkpoints: 17 airports, 27 land border gates and 39 seaports. Resolution 127/NQ-CP (August 2023) named the first 42; Resolution 389/NQ-CP (2 December 2025) added 41 more. The airports on the list include Noi Bai (Hanoi), Tan Son Nhat (Ho Chi Minh City), Da Nang, Cam Ranh (Nha Trang) and Phu Quoc. Overland, the list includes Moc Bai and Xa Mat on the Cambodia border and Lao Bao and Cau Treo on the Laos border.

Name the checkpoint where you plan to enter on the form — the embassy notes your actual entry checkpoint does not have to match it, but it must still be a designated international checkpoint.

The Phu Quoc exception — 30 days without a visa

There is one way for an Indian passport holder to visit Vietnam without a visa: Phu Quoc island. No visa is required if your entry and exit are both through Phu Quoc — arriving from abroad direct to the island — and you stay up to 30 days. The moment you go on to the mainland, you need an e-visa. Check with your airline before booking a connection through Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City.

The safe plan

Apply on evisa.gov.vn four weeks before departure, choose multiple entry (USD 50, about ₹4,650) if there is any chance of a side trip out of Vietnam, and do not book non-refundable flights or hotels until the printed e-visa is in your hand.

Questions, answered

Do Indians need a visa for Vietnam?

Yes — an ordinary Indian passport needs an e-visa before travelling. India is not on Vietnam’s 45-day visa-free lists. The only exception is Phu Quoc island, up to 30 days when you enter and exit through Phu Quoc itself.

How long does the Vietnam e-visa take?

Officially 3 working days, according to the Vietnam Immigration Department. The Embassy of Vietnam in India says 5–30 working days and advises applying at least 7–40 working days before departure. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding Vietnamese public holidays; there is no official express service.

How much is the Vietnam e-visa in rupees?

USD 25 single entry (about ₹2,325) or USD 50 multiple entry (about ₹4,650), at 1 USD ≈ ₹93 seen 24 November 2026. Non-refundable.

Can I get a visa on arrival in Vietnam?

Not as a general option for a holiday. A visa is only issued at the border in specific cases, with pre-approval arranged in advance through a Vietnamese sponsor. The e-visa is simpler and cheaper.
