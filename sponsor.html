Who reads us

Our readers are Indian families planning their first — or next — trip abroad, mostly from smaller cities and towns. They plan in rupees, travel with children and parents, and ask practical questions: what will it cost, where can we eat, what papers do we need. Many are Jain vegetarian travellers who need food planned properly, and many are Tirumala pilgrims planning a darshan trip. They come to us because the answers are plain, honest and in rupees.

We publish one article a day in Read, plus travel kits (₹999 each), free guides, and a flight search. Most of our readers are on phones — about three in four.

How sponsored items work

  • We write it. We never publish a sponsor’s own article, a coupon page, or a topic our readers do not want.
  • “Sponsored” is the first word the reader sees, at the very top of the item.
  • Links to the sponsor carry rel=“sponsored”.
  • Facts are checked like any article. The sponsor never controls our verdict — if the product is weak, we decline the deal.
  • Our founder, Shenick Jain, passes every sponsored item before it goes live.

What we refuse

We do not work with betting, loans, anything unlicensed, visa “guarantees”, or anyone with unresolved complaints. We also never recommend a product, sponsor or link because it pays — if the best answer for the reader earns us nothing, we give it anyway.

Questions, answered

How big is your audience, really?

Small, and we say so on this page with the dates. On 16 November 2026 we had 9 visits from 4 people, and several of those were our own testing. Google sent us 0 clicks in the 28 days to 14 November 2026. We are growing from zero, and our value to a sponsor today is the exact reader — a family planning a real trip — not a large number.

What does “Sponsored” mean on your site?

It means a business paid to support that item. The word “Sponsored” is the first thing the reader sees, and any link to the sponsor carries rel=“sponsored”. The words are still ours, the facts are still checked, and the sponsor does not control what we say.

Can I write the article myself?

No. We write every sponsored item ourselves, on a topic our readers want. If you send us your own article, we will not publish it.

How do I pay?

Prices are agreed for each sponsorship and written into a short agreement first. Shenick collects payment himself — talk to us on WhatsApp or email and we will send the details.
