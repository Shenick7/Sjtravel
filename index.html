      Questions, answered

      What is your correct website address?

      Our website is sjtravel.in — with no S at the end. Please do not add an S: sjtravels.in (with an S) is a different company, not SJ Tours and Travels, Ambur. Always check you are on sjtravel.in when you visit us.

      What exactly is inside a kit?

      A day-by-day plan with timings, a food guide for that country (where to eat and what to order), visa steps using official websites only, a full budget in rupees for 4 travellers, and a packing list. Page counts: Dubai 11, Thailand 11, Singapore 13, Malaysia 12, Sri Lanka 11, Vietnam 13.

      Can you plan vegetarian meals?

      Yes. Tell us how you eat and we plan around that. Each kit covers where to eat, what to order, and the hidden traps — like fish sauce and meat-based broths in Vietnam. On a package, vegetarian meals can be arranged on request.

      Can you book the full trip for us instead?

      Yes — that is our holiday package service. You get one flat, all-inclusive price with land costs broken down line by line. Vegetarian meals can be arranged on request. A planning fee of 5–10% applies and is adjusted against your package if you book with us. You pay it with your quote, before you book — and it comes off your package price if you book, so it is never on top of the flat price. Airfares are live and subject to change until ticketed.
