      Questions, answered

      What is your correct website address?

      Our website is sjtravel.in — with no S at the end. Please do not add an S: sjtravels.in (with an S) is a different company, not SJ Tours and Travels, Ambur. Always check you are on sjtravel.in when you visit us.

      How do I receive the kit after paying?

      On WhatsApp itself. Once your UPI payment is confirmed on the chat, we send the kit PDF on the same chat. Save it to your phone — it works offline for the whole trip.

      What exactly is inside a kit?

      A day-by-day plan with timings, a food guide for that country (where to eat and what to order, with Jain veg options), visa steps using official websites only, a full budget in rupees for 4 travellers, and a packing list. Page counts: Dubai 11, Thailand 11, Singapore 13, Malaysia 12, Sri Lanka 11, Vietnam 13.

      Are flights included in the kit budgets?

      No. Kit budgets cover land costs — stays, food, local travel and sightseeing. Flight prices change daily, so they are kept separate and honest. If you want, our Flight Finder service (₹199) can help — we hunt across options and send you the best-value flights we can find for your dates.

      We follow strict Jain food rules. Will the food guidance actually work abroad?

      Yes — tell us you need Jain food and we will plan for it. Each kit covers where Jain food is realistically available, the phrases to use when ordering, and the hidden traps — like fish sauce and meat-based broths in Vietnam. Where strict Jain meals need advance notice, the kit says so plainly, and on a package we can arrange Jain vegetarian food on request.

      Can you book the full trip for us instead?

      Yes — that is our holiday package service. You get one flat, all-inclusive price with land costs broken down line by line. Jain vegetarian food can be arranged on request. A planning fee of 5–10% applies and is adjusted against your package if you book with us. Airfares are live and subject to change until ticketed.

      Why are some visa forms in the kits “free”?

      Because they are. Forms like Singapore's SG Arrival Card and Thailand's TDAC cost nothing on the official website — look-alike sites charge for them. Every kit points you only to official sites, so you never pay for what is free.
