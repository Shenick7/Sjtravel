What it adds

Flights, as one figure for the whole family. Stay, as a nightly figure times the nights you type. Food, as a per-person daily figure times people times days. Local travel, per day for the family. Visas and arrival-card fees. Anything else you want in the pot: an eSIM, shopping, a buffer.

Questions, answered

Does this look up flight prices?

No. Type the fare you found, or leave it at zero.

Are children priced the same as adults for food?

Only if you type one food figure. The calculator multiplies that figure by adults plus children. If a child eats less, lower the figure or work it out yourself and type the family total into another box.

Where are the kit budgets?

On each kit page, labelled as land costs for 4 travellers, flights extra. They are planning bands from the kit, not live prices.

Can you build the budget for us?

Yes, on WhatsApp, as a holiday package or as a kit. The calculator stays free.
