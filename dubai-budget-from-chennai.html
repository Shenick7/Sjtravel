A Dubai budget from Chennai

This page will not tell you a hotel costs a particular number of rupees tonight. It tells you which lines to add, points at the band already printed on our Dubai kit, and sends you to live search for the rest.

The lines

  • Flights, Chennai to Dubai and back, for the actual dates. Use the flight search. The fare changes until it is ticketed.
  • Stay, per night, times nights. Search it yourself. A hotel in Bur Dubai or Karama is the practical base if you want Jain food, which is a separate guide.
  • Food, local taxis or the metro, a desert evening if you want one, and the visa or entry fee that applies to your passport on the day you apply.
  • An eSIM if you do not want home-network roaming, and a buffer for shopping. Those are choices, not a tariff.

The only rupee band on this page

The Dubai kit page publishes a land-cost band of ₹1,07,000–₹1,59,000 for 4 travellers, international flights extra. That is the kit’s planning band, written for the kit, not a fare we checked on 17 Nov 2026 and not a hotel quote.

Visa fees are not repeated here. The Dubai visa article on this site walks through documents. Re-read the official fee on the government page before you pay, because this budget page did not re-quote a dirham figure.

Use the family budget calculator. Type the quotes you found. The total is yours.
