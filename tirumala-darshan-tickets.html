Tirumala Darshan Tickets: How to Book and What They Cost

Tirumala darshan tickets are sold by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) — not by travel agents. Book only on the official portal, ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in, or the official TTDevasthanams app, using your own mail id and mobile number. TTD’s own terms: “TTD does not authorize any agents for online booking of Darshan / Seva / Accommodation / Prasadam.” SJ Tours does not sell darshan tickets, and no agent can book one for you.

The ticket types

How to book the ₹300 ticket, in 5 steps

  1. Create your TTD account before release day — on ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in, with your mobile number and OTP. Fill in your profile with your name, age and photo-ID number. Do not leave this for the morning of the release.
  2. Watch for TTD’s release announcement — TTD announces the month’s release dates a few days before, on the portal and on TTD news. The ₹300 quota usually opens around the 24th of the month at 10:00 AM IST, for dates about 3 months ahead.
  3. Log in just before 10:00 AM on release day — weekend and festival slots sell out within minutes. Go to Special Entry Darshan (₹300), pick your date and time slot.
  4. Add every pilgrim with their photo-ID number — the name on the ticket must match a government photo ID. Pay online.
  5. Download the ticket and carry the same original ID — printed or on your phone, plus the original ID you used at booking.

What no one can sell you

TTD’s terms begin: “TTD does not authorize any agents for online booking of Darshan / Seva / Accommodation / Prasadam.” And on the portal’s home page: “Devotees are requested to book Arjitha Seva / Darshan Tickets of your own, using your own mail id and mobile number. TTD does not support or encourage booking through agents.”

No travel agent or operator can book a darshan ticket for you — anyone promising a confirmed darshan slot inside a package is promising something TTD does not allow.

Before you travel

  • Check the current rules on ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in in the week you go. Release dates, quotas and rules change.
  • Book only on the official portal or the official app. Do not click ads for darshan tickets, and do not use look-alike sites — TTD has repeatedly warned about fake portals.
  • Carry the same original photo ID as on the ticket for every pilgrim. Children under 12 go free with their parents.
  • Arrive 30–45 minutes before your slotted time for Special Entry Darshan.
  • Vaikunta Ekadasi falls on 20 December 2026, and the Vaikunta Dwara stays open 20–29 December — expect the heaviest crowds of the year.
  • TTD call centre (24×7): 155257; helpdesk@tirumala.org.

Questions, answered

Do I need a ticket for free darshan?

No. Sarva Darshan is free and needs no booking — walk in at the Vaikuntam Queue Complex. The wait is the price: hours, longer in peak season.

Can I buy the ₹300 ticket when I reach Tirumala?

No — for the general public it is online only, on the official portal. The only on-arrival exception is serving defence personnel with their service ID.

How far ahead do tickets open?

About 3 months, in monthly batches. The exact release date is announced by TTD a few days before.

Can SJ Tours book the darshan ticket for me?

No — the ticket is booked by the pilgrim on the official portal, with their own mobile number, OTP and photo ID. What we can do is plan the rest of the trip around your darshan — travel from Ambur, Chennai or Bengaluru, a stay, and Jain food — ask on WhatsApp.
