Will your waitlist ticket confirm?

Indian Railways does not publish a confirmation percentage. Anyone who prints “GNWL confirms 80% of the time” is guessing. This page explains the status codes and the chart clock that a railway notification actually states.

The words on the PNR page

The passenger enquiry on indianrail.gov.in prints a legend. CNF means confirmed, with coach and berth after charting. RAC is reservation against cancellation. WL is a waiting-list number. GNWL is general wait list. PQWL is pooled quota wait list. RLWL is remote location wait list. TQWL is Tatkal quota wait list. RQWL is roadside quota wait list. REGRET means no more booking is permitted.

A North Western Railway note on reservation rules describes the order, without giving odds. Confirmed berths are allotted up to the quota. After that, RAC gives a confirmed seat, not a full berth, in the classes where RAC exists. A cancelled berth goes to RAC passengers before it goes to the waiting list. If a waiting-list ticket is still waiting when the chart is prepared, that note says the passenger travels only in an unreserved coach or cancels.

None of those documents says that a low GNWL number “usually confirms”. Do not plan a wedding or a darshan slot on a probability you saw on a blog.

When the first chart is prepared

Western Railway’s Advance Rates Notification No. 46 (Coaching) of 2025, dated 17 December 2025, quotes Railway Board letter No. 2024/TG-I/20/Reservation Chart of 16 December 2025. It says the first reservation chart shall be prepared preferably by 20:00 the previous day for departures from 05:01 to 14:00, and preferably at least 10 hours before departure for departures from 14:01 to 23:59 and from 00:00 to 05:00.

The word in that notification is “preferably”. It is a target the zones were told to aim at. The PNR page is where you see whether your ticket’s chart has actually been prepared. Berths can still change until the final chart. This page does not state a minute-count for the final chart, because that December notification does not.

Tatkal waitlist is a different ticket

TQWL is the Tatkal quota’s waiting list, as the PNR legend names it. IRCTC’s Tatkal FAQ says a confirmed Tatkal ticket is not refunded if you cancel it. A waitlisted Tatkal cancellation follows existing railway rules. The FAQ does not print a confirmation chance for TQWL, and neither does this page.

Check the live status on the PNR enquiry. The current status can move until the final chart.

Questions, answered

What does GNWL mean?

The indianrail.gov.in PNR legend expands GNWL as general wait list. It does not attach a confirmation percentage.

Is RAC a berth?

The North Western Railway reservation note says RAC allots a confirmed seat, and a cancelled berth is offered to RAC passengers before the waiting list. If no berth comes, the RAC passenger travels on the seat.

When will I know for sure?

After the chart for your train is prepared. The December 2025 notification sets the first-chart target. Read the chart status on your PNR.

Can you predict my chance?

No. We will not invent one. If the train will not do, the Flight Finder is a separate ₹199 search, or you can look at Tatkal rules for the next day.
