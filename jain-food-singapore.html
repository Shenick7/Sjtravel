Jain food in Singapore

Singapore has Indian vegetarian restaurants. That is not the same as a kitchen that already cooks your family’s Jain rules. Say the full list, every meal.

Say all of it

“Jain food, please. No onion, no garlic, no potato, carrot or other root vegetables. No egg. Please cook it fresh.”

Sambar, chutney and a “veg” gravy are the usual places onion and garlic hide. Ask about those, not only about the main dish.

We did not confirm a current Jain menu on a Singapore restaurant’s own website on 17 Nov 2026, so this page names none. A blog that says a kitchen “can accommodate Jain requests” is not a check.

Little India is the practical start

Serangoon Road is where Indian grocery shops and vegetarian restaurants are concentrated. Start there for an Indian vegetarian meal, then use the sentence above. Being in Little India does not mean the default dish is Jain.

Questions, answered

Can we trust a vegetarian sign?

It rules meat out. It does not rule out onion, garlic, egg or potato. Ask.
