Visa-free country finder

Type a country. The list is the MEA visa-free and visa-on-arrival tables read on 17 Nov 2026, with Thailand corrected to the Thai government’s 30-day tourist exemption. Nothing you type is sent anywhere except to load that file.

How to read a row

“Visa-free” and “visa on arrival” are MEA’s headings. The note under a country is MEA’s remarks, unless the sources on that row include a destination government page. Thailand’s row does. An empty note means MEA’s cell was empty. It does not mean there are no conditions.

The written explanation, including Nepal, Bhutan, Malaysia and the UAE’s conditional visa on arrival, is on the guide.

Questions, answered

Where do the countries come from?

The Ministry of External Affairs page for ordinary Indian passports, visa-free and visa-on-arrival tables, read on 17 Nov 2026.

Why is Thailand 30 days?

Because the Royal Thai Embassy in New Delhi says the 30-day tourist exemption took effect on 15 September 2026. MEA still showed 60 days.

Why is Singapore missing?

It is not on those two MEA tables. It is on MEA’s separate e-visa table. E-visa countries are not in this finder.

Does this file an application?

No. Visa help, if you need it, is a separate conversation on WhatsApp.
