Jain food in Colombo

Sri Lanka is an easy flight from Chennai and a hard kitchen if you assume “vegetable” means Jain. Ask what went into the pot. This page does not name a Colombo restaurant, because none was verified from its own site on 17 Nov 2026.

The question that matters

“Jain food, please. No onion, no garlic, no potato or other root vegetables. No egg. No fish sauce, dried fish or shrimp paste. Cook it fresh.”

A board that says “vegetable curry” is not enough. Ask whether that pot is finished with dried fish or Maldive fish, which is common in Sri Lankan cooking even when the main ingredient is a vegetable. If the person serving you cannot say, pick another dish.

Indian vegetarian restaurants are the easier start, and you still say the Jain sentence. We are not printing names we did not check.

Questions, answered

Is vegetable curry safe?

Only if you have asked about dried fish, shrimp paste, onion, garlic and egg. The word vegetable does not answer those.

Why no restaurant names?
