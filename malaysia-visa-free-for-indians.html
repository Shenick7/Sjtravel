Malaysia visa-free entry for Indians

Thirty days, until 31 December 2026, for tourism, business, a social visit or transit. Not for taking a job. Malaysia’s foreign ministry in New Delhi prints the conditions, and so does MEA.

The waiver

MEA’s visa-free table lists Malaysia as 30 days until 31 December 2026, and points at the Malaysian High Commission in New Delhi.

Malaysia’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs in New Delhi says the waiver runs until 31 December 2026, for up to 30 days, for business, tourism, social visits or transit. Immigration’s country list marks India as visa-exempt until that date.

The High Commission of India in Kuala Lumpur has warned that the waiver is not for employment, and that people who arrive without the documents below are refused and sent back on the airline that brought them.

What to have ready

  • A passport valid at least six months.
  • A confirmed return ticket.
  • A confirmed hotel booking.
  • The Malaysia Digital Arrival Card, submitted online within three days before arrival, at the immigration MDAC page. It is the government’s form. A site that charges you to “process” it is not the form.

Questions, answered

How long can we stay?

Up to 30 days, until the waiver’s end date of 31 December 2026, for tourism, business, a social visit or transit.

Is it free?

The waiver itself is not a visa you pay Malaysia for. You still need the MDAC, a return ticket and a hotel booking. Flights and hotels are your own cost.

Can we look for work on this entry?

No. India’s High Commission in Kuala Lumpur says the waiver is not for employment, and that people who use it that way are refused or become illegal after 30 days.

Where is the MDAC?

https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main — the immigration department’s form, within three days before you arrive.
