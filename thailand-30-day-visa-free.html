Thailand’s 30-Day Visa-Free Rule for Indians

The rule changed on 15 September 2026: Indian passport holders now enter Thailand visa-free for up to 30 days — not 60. Here is the rule in plain words, the one form you must not miss, and what immigration can ask for.

The rule, in plain words

Indian passport holders can enter Thailand visa-free for tourism for up to 30 days. There is no visa fee and no embassy visit for a normal family holiday.

The limit used to be longer, until 15 September 2026, when Thailand cut it to 30 days. A family holiday of 6 to 10 days is well inside the limit — just keep your return ticket date correct.

Entry rules can change, so re-check the rule one week before you fly.

TDAC — the one form you must not miss

Since 1 May 2025, every foreign visitor must fill the free Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) online before arrival.

  • Fill it within 72 hours before you land, for every person including children.
  • Only on the official site: tdac.immigration.go.th.
  • It is free. Any website charging money for the TDAC is a fake — close it.
  • Keep the confirmation on your phone and as a screenshot.

What immigration can ask for

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months from your entry date, with a blank page.
  • Return or onward flight ticket out of Thailand.
  • Hotel booking proof for your stay.
  • Proof of funds — officially about THB 20,000 per person or THB 40,000 per family, in cash or bank proof. It is not checked for everyone, but carry it.
  • TDAC confirmation for every traveller.

Documents checklist — pack one folder

  • Passports for all travellers + one photocopy set + phone photos of each passport.
  • Flight tickets — international and Bangkok to Phuket domestic.
  • Hotel vouchers for Bangkok and Phuket / Pattaya.
  • Travel insurance papers — not compulsory for entry, but strongly recommended for medical cover.
  • Debit / credit card that works internationally (turn on international use in your bank app before you fly).
  • Passport-size photos — 2 per person, useful for any form on the trip.
  • For children travelling with one parent: carry a consent letter from the other parent.

Do not overstay

Do not overstay your visa-free entry. Overstay is fined per day and can affect future travel. A family holiday of 6 to 10 days is well inside the 30-day limit — just keep your return ticket date correct.

Questions, answered

How long can Indians stay in Thailand visa-free?

Up to 30 days for tourism, since the rule changed on 15 September 2026. Any page or video still quoting the old, longer limit is out of date.

Do I need a visa at all?

No, not for a normal family holiday. Indian passport holders enter visa-free, with no visa fee and no embassy visit. You do still need the free TDAC form.

What is the TDAC and when do I fill it?

The Thailand Digital Arrival Card. Fill it free, online, within 72 hours before you land, for every traveller including children, only at tdac.immigration.go.th. Keep the confirmation on your phone and as a screenshot.

A website is asking me to pay for the TDAC. Is it real?

No. The TDAC is free. Any website charging money for it is a fake — close it and use only tdac.immigration.go.th.

What documents should I carry?

Passport (6+ months validity), return ticket, hotel booking proof, proof of funds, and your TDAC confirmation. The Thailand kit has the full documents checklist and the 6-day plan.
